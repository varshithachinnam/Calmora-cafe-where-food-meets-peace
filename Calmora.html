Most cafés mix everything in one room. Calmora separates quiet work, reading, conversation and food.

Noise-controlled seats with charging points and stable Wi-Fi for study, assignments and long work sessions.

Reading corner

A dedicated spot with books for a calm break between study blocks.

Social tables

Group discussions and project meetings without disturbing people who are focusing.

Food and beverages

Quality food and drinks served at your table, so you can stay productive.

Why it is needed

Cafés are a gamble. Calmora is not.

A 2025 study found students split almost evenly on whether cafés help or hurt their focus.

48.2%of students faced distractions in cafés (2025 study)
50.4%found cafés calm and peaceful, so results vary from place to place
13 of 15people who tried our prototype liked or loved it

Prototype visitors liked the peaceful atmosphere, comfortable seating, food and the reading corner. They asked for more quiet seats and more privacy for online meetings, so we are planning for both.
